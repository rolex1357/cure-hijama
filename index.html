What is Hijama Cupping Therapy?

Hijama, or wet cupping therapy, is an ancient Sunnah-based healing practice trusted across cultures for centuries. Cups are placed on the skin to create suction, drawing out stagnant blood and toxins and encouraging the body's natural healing.

At CURE Hijama & Healing Centre, every session is performed using sterile, single-use equipment in a clean and professional environment.

  • Relief from back, neck, shoulder and joint pain
  • Improved blood circulation and detoxification
  • Reduced muscle tension and stress
  • Support for overall wellbeing and immunity
  • Traditional Sunnah practice — rooted in Islamic heritage
⚠

Hijama is a complementary wellness practice and not a substitute for professional medical advice. Clients with existing health conditions should consult a qualified healthcare professional before booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hijama / cupping therapy? +
Hijama is an ancient healing practice rooted in Sunnah where cups are placed on the skin to create suction, promoting blood flow and detoxification. Wet cupping additionally involves small superficial incisions to remove stagnant blood.
Is Hijama painful? +
Most patients feel mild pressure or a pulling sensation. Wet cupping may cause slight discomfort but is generally well-tolerated. Our practitioners ensure your comfort throughout.
How many sessions will I need? +
It depends on your condition. Some see relief after 1–2 sessions; chronic conditions may need 4–6 sessions. Our practitioners will guide you with a personalised plan.
Are sessions gender-separated? +
Yes. Rizwan Ulla Khan treats male patients only (8951806198) and Mrs. Fazila Khan treats female patients only (7892204394).
Are the instruments sterilised? +
Absolutely. All cups are sterilised before each session and single-use blades are used for wet cupping, ensuring complete safety for every patient.
Do I need to book in advance? +
We strongly recommend booking ahead as slots fill quickly. Simply call or WhatsApp us to reserve your session.
